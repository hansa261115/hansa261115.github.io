Privacy Policy

This policy describes how user information is handled in this offline recording app (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information from users. Recorded audio data is stored only on the user's device and is never sent to the developer's servers. The App does not communicate with any external servers.

2. Use of the Microphone

The App uses the microphone solely for its recording feature. Recording takes place only when the user performs a recording operation.

3. Sharing with Third Parties, Advertising, and Analytics

The App does not provide recorded data or any other information to third parties. The App contains no advertising and no analytics mechanisms for tracking usage.

4. Exporting Data

When a user exports recorded data using the sharing features or similar functions, the data is sent to the destination the user has chosen, through the user's own action.

5. In-App Purchases

Purchases of paid features are processed by Apple. Payment information, such as credit card details, is never shared with the developer.

6. Deleting Data

When the App is deleted from a device, the recorded data stored on that device is deleted along with it.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
